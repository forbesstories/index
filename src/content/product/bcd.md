The BCP X500 FA20 Turbocharger allows you to take your 2015-2021 WRX to the next level. With its ability to produce over 400whp on the stock engine, it makes it a suitable upgrade for vehicles that already have intake and exhaust modifications.

The X500 is designed to work with both stock-engine and built-engine applications, offering over 450+ Whp potential when paired with a properly built engine setup.

The BCP X500 FA20 Turbocharger is designed as a drop-in unit and works with factory-style inlets and J-Pipes.

It includes a billet internal wastegate actuator along with wastegate springs, providing a straightforward installation approach for compatible applications.
